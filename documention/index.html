Installation 01

FTP Upload

  1. Connect: Open your FTP manager and connect to your hosting server.
  2. Navigate: Browse to the required directory (typically public_html).
  3. Upload: Upload all files from the Stynero folder to your server.

After upload, your site will be live at your domain. For local development, use a tool like XAMPP or MAMP.
